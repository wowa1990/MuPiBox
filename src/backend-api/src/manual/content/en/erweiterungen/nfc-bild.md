# NFC: Changing the picture of a tag

In the list of tags every tag shows a picture. By default it is the **cover of the medium**, as the tile shows it too: an album with `cover.jpg`, the picture from Spotify, the picture of a podcast, the logo of a station. You can give the tag a **picture of its own**, for example a photo of the child or a drawing you stick on the tag.

## Changing the picture

You can choose the picture in two places:

- **Right when assigning:** in the window that waits for the tag tap **Choose image** (or **Change picture**).
- **Later:** in the library in the **NFC tag** window of the medium, or on the page [Assigned tags](nfc-zugeordnete-tags.md), tap **Change picture** at the tag.

The **Cover** window offers two ways:

![The window to choose a picture](nfc-image.png)

1. **Search:** the box searches **iTunes, Deezer and Spotify** for the name of the medium; you can change the search term. Tap a picture, then **Apply**. The search term goes to Apple, Deezer and Spotify for this.
2. **Own image:** **Own image** picks an image from the device (photo, file). It is cropped to a square.

## Back to the cover of the medium

If a tag has a picture of its own, the button **Use the cover of the medium** (arrow back) is no longer greyed out at it on the page **Assigned tags**. That gives it the picture of the medium again.

## Where the picture is kept

The box keeps a picture of your own in its folder for own pictures (the address starts with `/cover/`) and shows it in the list of tags and in the **NFC tag** window. It does not change the cover of the album itself.
